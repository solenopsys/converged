## What Converged Does

A manufacturing company has two sides of its work. The first is making the part, assembling the product, and completing the order. The second is everything that surrounds production: receiving requests, handling files, agreeing on prices, scheduling jobs, communicating with customers, monitoring equipment, managing materials, tracking deadlines, sending notifications, and getting paid.

**Converged takes care of this second side.** It is a Smart Workshop platform that connects AI, business processes, equipment, people, and external services into one system. The owner no longer has to piece together information from websites, chats, spreadsheets, machine interfaces, and separate business tools. Converged provides a live view of the business: what came in, what was accepted, what is in the queue, what is ready, where delays are developing, and where money or time is being lost.

Converged is especially useful when production already works, but the business around it still depends on chats, spreadsheets, employee memory, and manual coordination. It does not require a company to replace everything with a large ERP or MES. Instead, it starts with concrete processes and gradually builds a connected system around the way the company actually works.

In a typical scenario, a customer submits a request through a website, form, messenger, or operator. Converged stores the request, attaches the files, creates the order, helps estimate the price, schedules the work, tracks execution, and keeps the customer informed. An operator can ask for the current status through the interface or AI chat, and receive an answer based on actual system and equipment data.

AI can also handle routine actions within the same business context: prepare customer responses, identify delays, collect information from different systems, update statuses, launch permitted Workflows, and prepare the next actions for an operator. The system does not simply generate text — it works with the actual data and operations of the workshop.

As a result, the workshop becomes less dependent on manual coordination and individual employee memory. **People focus on production, engineering, and decisions that require expertise. Converged takes care of everything around them.**
